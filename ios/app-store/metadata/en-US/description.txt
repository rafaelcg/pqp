pqp is open source voice and text chat for your group, Discord-style: communities with servers, voice and text channels, direct messages, and a public directory to discover new ones.

A watch party already drew over 100 people watching together in one room, in September 2026. Free, no ads, and the full source is public on GitHub.

VOICE AND VIDEO
Join a voice channel and talk with clear audio: a peer-to-peer connection for small groups, or a dedicated media server for bigger rooms, picked automatically by the app so you never have to think about it. Turn your camera on, watch someone else's shared screen, see who is speaking, mute or deafen yourself, and rate the call quality when you hang up. Ring a friend directly for a one-on-one call.

TEXT CHAT
Send messages with reactions, replies, threads, pinned messages and GIFs. Paste a link and get a preview. Search a whole server's history. Messages you send sync instantly to everyone else in the channel, on any platform.

WATCH PARTIES
Join a watch party already in progress and watch the shared stream alongside the room's chat, with the video picking up right where the group is.

COMMUNITIES
Browse a public directory of communities and join the ones that interest you with one tap. Every community has its own text and voice channels, just like a private server.

FRIENDS AND PROFILES
Add friends by their tag or their public handle, see who is online, and block anyone you would rather not hear from. Claim your own handle to get a shareable profile page.

PRIVACY AND MODERATION
Report a message, a person or a community in a couple of taps. Server owners get kick, ban, timeout and an audit log. Delete your account and export your data whenever you want.

OPEN SOURCE
pqp is free, has no ads, and its full source is public on GitHub (rafaelcg/pqp) under the AGPL license. Anyone can read the code, self-host their own instance, or contribute.

This build is a beta. A few things are still catching up to the web app: group DMs, raising your hand in a voice call, and starting your own watch party are not on iOS yet, though they already work on pqp's website and desktop app. We ship improvements often, iOS included.
