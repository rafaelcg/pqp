# Forum channels: pre-launch QA report

2026-10-12. QA gate before `forum_channels` goes on for QG.

## Verdict

**Yes. Forum is ready to turn on for QG once #1101 to #1105 merge.** I found no blockers. Ten should-fix defects all have PRs: five from my pass, and five from Rafael's live test, which were added mid-run. 23 polish items are listed below and none of them holds the launch.

The two must-merge PRs are **#1101** and **#1103**. #1101 fixes a published post's body coming back as the next post's draft. #1103 stops raw `**markdown**` from showing on every RSS-feed card. #1105 covers Rafael's own complaints: thin cards beside an open post, no video thumbnail, small titles, and double click.
