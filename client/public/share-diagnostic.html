pqp share diagnostic

Finds where a screen share loses frames on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere: the video goes from this page to itself.

  1. Open the game (or whatever you share) the way you normally play it.
  2. Press Start and pick the same screen or window you would share in pqp.
  3. Go back to the game and play normally for at least 30 seconds.
  4. Come back here and press Stop. Read the verdict, then press Copy report.

Rows shaded blue are seconds when this page was not in front (you were in the game). Those are the ones that matter. A still picture sends few frames on purpose, so keep the game moving while it measures.

No game on this computer? Open the stand-in game: a window that keeps the graphics card as busy as an uncapped game. Share that window.

On a Mac the browser needs permission to record the screen: System Settings, Privacy and Security, Screen and System Audio Recording, turn on your browser, then quit and reopen it.
