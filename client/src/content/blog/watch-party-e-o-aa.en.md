A lot landed. Watch party, formatting in chat, shortcuts, and a mic that closes when you stop talking.

## Watch party

Join the call. On the bar, **Watch party**. Chrome asks for a tab. Pick YouTube, or the player. That tab's sound goes with it.

![Watch party on the call bar](/blog/watch-party-e-o-aa/watch-party.webp "Watch party. Share the tab in Chrome, with that tab's audio.")

To stop: the same control.

## Aa, and Preview

In the composer, **Aa** (Formatting). Bold, Italic, Strikethrough, Code, a fence, a quote, a list.

Tap the same one again and it unwraps. Underneath is **Preview**: how it will look in the bubble. The field still has the `**`.

![Aa open, Bold in the composer](/blog/watch-party-e-o-aa/formatacao.webp "Aa on. Bold. The field keeps **negrito**.")

On a phone, Aa is in the + menu. Cmd+B, Cmd+I, Cmd+E and Cmd+Shift+X work too.

## Shortcuts

Cmd+/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows) opens the map. Mute is Shift+Cmd+M. Deafen is Shift+Cmd+D. They fire even while you are typing in chat.

![The Shortcuts map](/blog/watch-party-e-o-aa/atalhos.webp "Keyboard, Shortcuts. Mute, Deafen, channels. Change them in Settings.")

Settings, **Keyboard**: the same bindings. **View map** opens it again.

## The mic closes

Settings, **Voice & Video**. The line on the meter is **Sensitivity**. Above it, the mic opens. Quiet, the bar says **Quiet**. Talking, **Live**.

![Sensitivity on the meter](/blog/watch-party-e-o-aa/sensibilidade.webp "Voice & Video. The line on the meter. Above it, the mic opens.")

A tab in the background keeps measuring.

## Camera and share on the strip

Under **Voice connected**: **Turn camera on** and **Share screen**. Two equal tiles. Hang-up stays on the header. Mute and deafen stay on your row.

## Drag people on a call

Under **Voice**, the seated name. Drag them onto another voice channel. Right-click: Mute for me, Kick from the community, View profile.

Drop on a text channel does nothing. If the mic was muted, it stays muted.

## Speak and Video

The channel cog, **Channel settings**. **Speak** is the microphone. **Video** is camera and screen. You can let someone talk with no camera. It survives a refresh.

![Speak and Video in permissions](/blog/watch-party-e-o-aa/falar-video.webp "Speak is the mic. Video is camera and share.")

No Speak means they join muted and stay muted.

## Pin a channel

A channel way down the list. Right-click, **Pin**. It goes up to **Pinned**. Drag to reorder. **Unpin** puts it back in its category. Only for you.

## View profile

If they have a handle: on the card, **View profile**. Opens `pqp.gg/@name` in a new tab. No handle, no button.

## The rest

- Communities: a broken icon falls back to initials. **Join** / **Open**.
- On the roster, **Search members**. The X clears it. Friends sit above Online.
- Kick, delete a channel, leave: the in-app dialog.
- Status pips on messages follow the member list.
- On a call: up to three bars. **Relayed** when the path is TURN.
- A crowded room: you join muted.
- Slow mode on the channel: Channel settings.
- Share volume is one slider. The presenter's voice is another.
- Done watching someone's screen: stop only yours. The call stays.
- Mid-call, the site will not ask you to reload.
- The first time, the app points at Aa, Watch party, Pin, and Cmd+/.
- [pqp.gg/download](/download): a Windows portable, for when the installer will not open.
- [pqp.gg/android](/android): today's APK joins a LiveKit call.
