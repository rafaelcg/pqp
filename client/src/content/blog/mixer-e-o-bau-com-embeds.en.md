Saturday's watch party pulled in over 500 people watching together. The whole week went into making that tool solid: an audio mixer for the host, quality you pick yourself, a checklist before going live, and fullscreen everywhere people watch.

## The stream mixer

The **Transmissão** panel, which opens once you're live, now has a **Mixer do stream**. Two controls in dB: **Meu mic** and **Áudio da aba**. Turn the mic up if the film is drowning you out, turn the tab down if it's the other way. **Padrão** resets to the default, and an output meter shows what's actually reaching viewers.

It also adjusts itself: while you're talking, the tab audio ducks a little and comes back the moment you stop. Nobody has to babysit a slider mid-stream.

## Quality, your call

Same panel, **Qualidade da transmissão**: 720p (the default, recommended) or 1080p. It applies to the next time you go live, the stream already running keeps whatever it had. The choice is saved to your account and resets to 720p on every new watch party, so nobody forgets a 1080p setting on a connection that can't carry it.

Underneath, capture already runs up to 60 frames a second when the picked quality can take it.

## Before you go live

Setting up a watch party, next to the **Compartilhar tela** button, there's a checklist called **Antes de ir ao vivo**: browser (Chrome or another Chromium, Firefox can't share a tab safely here), tab audio, quality, camera, mic. Nothing else blocks you, only Firefox does: it stops you from going live at all.

If the stream carries no sound for more than 10 seconds, a warning shows up. And if your mic is muted while you're presenting, a banner sits on the bar until you click **Ativar mic**. Both of those already happened in a real call, so now they warn you before they turn into a chat complaint.

## Fullscreen everywhere

Fullscreen works on web, on iPhone Safari and in the iPhone app. In the app, the whole phone goes fullscreen, no button to hunt for.

## Viewers know what they're watching

A fixed badge on the player: **Ao vivo · ~Xs de atraso**. Hover it and it explains itself: that's the normal streaming delay, so everyone sees the same thing together.

While no picture is coming through, the holding screen now says why: the stream stalled and is reconnecting, it restarted with a countdown, or the host just hasn't shared anything yet. Before, it was always the same frozen screen.

Rooms over 10 people: join and leave sounds mute themselves. Want them back, there's a button on the call bar.

## Watching is not joining a call

Anyone just watching clicks **Assistir** and that's it: no microphone, no joining a call. The "Entrar na call" button is gone for the audience; what's left is **Pedir pra falar**, if the host left voice on. Host and co-hosts keep their own stage.

Leave the channel mid-watch-party and it doesn't stop: it moves to a corner of the screen, still playing. Browse other voice channels and it stays right there.

## Baú got embeds

Paste a Twitch link (channel, VOD or clip), a TikTok or an Instagram link into a Baú post and it becomes an embed, with a preview already showing in the composer as you type. Baú also got an unread counter and a corner toast when something new lands, without pulling you away from what you were looking at.

## Music in calls

Any voice call now has a music queue: paste a YouTube or Spotify link (a song, an album, a playlist) or search by name, and everyone on the call hears it. Anyone who can speak adds music and can remove what they added; skipping, reordering and stopping the queue takes **Gerenciar música**.

## The rest

- Screen sharing: system audio is now one checkbox in Chrome's own picker, and the call no longer leaks into whatever you share.
- A watch party no longer starts without a picture picked first.
- Doubled mic audio during a stream: fixed. And opening the same watch party on two devices now warns you.
- A promoted co-host can actually take over presenting now. The button existed before and did nothing.
- `/clear` now actually purges the channel, with the same confirmation as the channel menu. Before, it only cleared what you'd typed.
- Advanced noise suppression: Settings, Voice & Video, Microphone processing, **Advanced (RNNoise)**. Cuts keyboard, dog and room echo, costs a bit more CPU. Off by default. Web only for now.
- Your draft is saved per channel. Switch channels and come back, what you were writing is still there.

## Not yet

Presenting a watch party from the desktop app: the code already does it, it just needs a new build cut. Today's download is still 0.1.5.
