Saturday's watch party had over 500 people watching together. This week was about making that tool solid: a mixer for the host, a quality you pick, a checklist before going live, and fullscreen everywhere you watch.

## The stream mixer

In the **Transmissão** panel, two controls: **My mic** and **Tab audio**. Raise one, lower the other, **Default** resets. A meter shows what is going out to the audience.

And when you talk, the tab audio dips on its own and comes back when you stop. No slider fiddling mid-film.

## Quality, your call

Same panel: 720p (default) or 1080p. It applies the next time you go live and resets to 720p for every new watch party, so nobody leaves 1080p on over a connection that cannot carry it. Capture now reaches 60 fps when the quality allows.

## Before going live

Next to **Share screen**, a quick checklist: browser, tab audio, quality, camera, mic. Only Firefox blocks, because it cannot share a tab safely.

Stream silent for more than 10 seconds? A warning shows. Mic muted while you present? A strip stays on the bar until you unmute. Both happened live, so now they warn before the chat does.

## Fullscreen everywhere

Web, iPhone Safari and the iPhone app. In the app, the whole phone becomes the screen.

## Viewers know what they are seeing

The waiting screen now says why: reconnecting, restarting (with a countdown), or the host has not shared yet.

Rooms above 10 people mute their join and leave sounds on their own. One button brings them back.

## Watching is not joining the call

Click **Watch** and that is it: no microphone, no call. What is left is **Ask to speak**, when the host allows voice. Host and co-hosts keep their stage.

Left the channel? The watch party moves to a corner and keeps playing.

## Baú got embeds

A Twitch link (channel, VOD or clip), TikTok or Instagram becomes an embed, with a preview while you type. And Baú tells you when something new lands: an unread counter and a corner toast.

## Music in the call

Every voice call has a music queue: paste a YouTube or Spotify link, or search by name, and everyone hears it together. Speakers add; skipping and reordering belong to **Manage music**.

## The rest

- Screen share: system audio is one checkbox in the picker, and the call no longer leaks into what you share.
- A watch party only starts with a picture chosen.
- Host voice doubled in the stream: fixed. Opened the same watch party on two devices? A warning tells you.
- A co-host can now really take over the presentation.
- `/clear` really purges the channel, with confirmation.
- Optional advanced noise suppression: Settings, Voice and video, **Advanced (RNNoise)**. Removes keyboard, dog and room echo. Web only for now.
- Drafts are kept per channel.
