## Voice messages

When nothing is typed, the send button becomes a microphone. On a phone, hold to record and let go to send. Slide left to cancel, slide up to lock and go hands-free. On a computer, click to start recording, pause if you need to and use **Listen first** to check it before sending. Esc discards (with a 5 second Undo) and Enter sends. Each message can run up to 5 minutes.

A voice message arrives as a card with a waveform, and you can play it at 1x, 1.5x or 2x. On your own messages you can see who has listened.

On a computer you can record a voice message in the middle of a call without touching the call's microphone. On iPhone, with a call open, the app does not record.

### The text of a voice message

The text shows under the message. When it does not come by itself, there is a **Transcribe** button. For that, the audio goes through a transcription service. The sender can turn it off, and the receiver can hide every transcript, in Settings, Privacy, Voice messages. Turning it off applies to the next messages you send.

The text shows on the website and in the desktop app. On iPhone the message records and plays, but the text does not show yet.

## Forward

You can forward a message from a channel or a thread, or a Baú post, to other channels and conversations. The message menu has **Forward**, and every Baú post has a matching button under it. Pick up to 5 destinations by searching their name, add a comment if you want and send. On the website the destinations include threads.

It arrives as a card that says where it came from, with the title, a piece of the text, who wrote it and the thumbnail. Tapping the card opens the original. The card itself does not mention anybody.

A message from a private channel and a post for VIP members only can be forwarded inside the same server only. A DM message cannot be forwarded. It works on the website and on iPhone.

## Share a Baú post

A free Baú post from a community with a public address gets a page anyone can open without an account: `pqp.gg/c/<community>/p/<post>`. Whoever lands there sees the post and a button to join the community.

Members get a **Share** button on the post: copy the link, X, Reddit, WhatsApp, Telegram or the phone's share sheet. The link shows with a cover and title on social networks. On the website and on iPhone.

## Server emoji

Whoever has the Manage emoji permission (the owner and Admins already do) uploads images in Server settings, Emoji: PNG, JPG or WebP, each with a name. Everyone in the server uses them by typing `:name:` (type `:po` and it suggests `:pog:`) or from the emoji picker, in the **From the server** row. An emoji only works in its own server, and a deleted emoji shows as `:name:`.

It works on the website and in the desktop app.

## Soundboard in calls

In a call you can play a short sound for the whole room. 6 sounds come built in, and the server can add up to 6 of its own (mp3 or ogg, up to 5.2 seconds). Each device plays the sound itself, so it does not go into the call's audio or into a recording. Each person can silence anyone's soundboard from their own audio menu. Whoever cannot speak in that room (muted by a moderator, no permission, mic locked) cannot play sounds either.

## Nudge

In a 1:1 DM with a friend there is a **Nudge** button. On the other side the conversation shakes once and a little knock plays. There is a cooldown between nudges. If you use reduced motion, the border blinks instead of shaking. On the website and on iPhone.

## Fullscreen and 60 fps

Screen share with the frame rate on Auto now captures at 60 fps in ordinary calls, on the website and in the desktop app. Before, if the open server had watch parties turned on, Auto dropped to 30 even in an ordinary voice call. In a watch party the stream's own limit still applies.

And when you watch in fullscreen, the app now asks for the picture at the screen's real resolution. On a laptop with Windows at 150% scaling, fullscreen used to ask for only 720p.

## Public servers ask first

When you open a server to anyone, by turning on the public address or the directory, pqp now asks first: anyone will be able to join and see every channel that is not restricted. The button starts on Cancel. A public server gets a **Public** badge under its name and on the left rail. On the website.

## Your servers in your order

Drag servers on the left rail to reorder them. On iPhone it is from the server's menu, with move up and move down. The order is yours, it is saved on your account and shows the same on your devices. A new server goes to the end, next to the +.

## Appearance

In Settings, Appearance & Language, there are three community palettes (Ocean, Catppuccin and Everforest) and a Halloween look, until November 2, with a card that lets you try it and undo. The Christmas one turns itself on on December 1.

## The rest

- When someone shares a screen, whoever watches has a **Picture-in-picture** button, on the screen's card and in the call bar, to keep watching from another tab or app. Computers only.
- The music player has a captions button and an Open on YouTube.
- Text and Voice in the sidebar fold, like categories do. Folded, it shows how many channels it hid and a dot if something is new.
- In a call, the bottom bar got a **More** menu (watch party, hide the mouse, join and leave sounds, fullscreen). Each picture has one row of controls, with the name, sound, more options and fullscreen.
- Camera, share and disconnect are back in the call card, on the side.
- Hearing again with Ctrl+Shift+D no longer opens your microphone too: it goes back to how it was before you deafened.
- A message from someone who left the server gets a "left" tag beside the name.
- In a channel where you cannot send messages, a read-only notice shows in place of the box. And the "somebody is typing..." line goes away as soon as the message arrives.
- The Settings windows are wider.
- App updates: a small icon with a dot, and the new version notice comes once a day. Only a truly critical version blocks the screen, and an open call never reloads by itself.
- Desktop app 0.2.7: right-click a misspelled word to get correction suggestions and Add to dictionary, and text fields have a Cut, Copy and Paste menu. The spellchecker knows Portuguese and English. And the banner outside the app plays the system sound when pqp does not play its own.
