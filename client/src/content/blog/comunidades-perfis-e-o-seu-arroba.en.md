Until now pqp was somewhere you sent a link and hoped. This week it got a face.

## Communities

A server is now a **community**, and that is not just a rename. A community can appear in a public directory, carry a category, a tagline and a picture, and be found by people who do not yet know anybody inside it.

Each one gets its own address: `pqp.gg/c/name`. That page is a poster, not a member list. Name, tagline, category, how many people, pictures. Never who is inside.

## Profiles and your @

You can claim a real `@` and become `pqp.gg/@you`. It is unique, first come first served, and you can change it once every 30 days.

The page shows your name, picture, a banner if you upload one, badges for the public communities you are in, and the month you joined. No email, no id, no presence.

## Depoimentos

People can write depoimentos on each other's profiles. The six newest approved ones appear on the public page. You approve them first, so nobody writes rubbish on your profile without going through you.

## Invites became links

It used to be a code you copied, pasted and explained. Now it is a link. On a phone it opens the native share sheet, so it reaches WhatsApp in two taps.

## A second language

The site and the app speak English now too. It follows your browser's language, and you can override it.

## Watching a screen share on iPhone

The iPhone app can now display a shared screen. Watching, not yet broadcasting.
