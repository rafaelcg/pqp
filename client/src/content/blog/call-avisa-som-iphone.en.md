This week was about making calls solid. A call rings you now, the sound is back on iPhone, and a few bugs you reported are gone.

## The call rings you

A call came in while the app was in the background, or you had turned off in-app notifications? Before, nothing. You missed it.

Now it rings and shows up, even with the window minimized. A call is a call: it reaches you either way, without depending on a setting you flipped earlier.

## On iPhone, the sound is back

A DM call on iPhone had video but no voice. The person showed up, moved their mouth, and no sound came out. Fixed.

And a call that used to ring twice on screen now rings once. It is in the latest TestFlight build.

## Watch party and calls, steadier

Three things you wrote in, now fixed:

- **Watch party:** the music played for one person and not another, ended for one and kept going for another. Now everyone hears the same thing at the same time.
- **Call:** you could see the same person twice in the room. No more doubles.
- **Call:** it dropped when two people shared their screen at once. It does not drop anymore.
