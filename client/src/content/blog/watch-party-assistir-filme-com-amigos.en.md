To watch a film together online with friends on pqp: join a voice call, click **Watch party**, pick the tab the film is in, and go live. Everyone in the call sees and hears the same film at the same instant, with an open mic to react to it.

This has already run with over 500 people watching at the same time. The steps below work whether it is four friends on a call or a large session.

## What you need before you start

- A pqp account and a community or server with a voice channel. [Create one in under a minute](/garanta) if you do not have one yet.
- To **present** the film from a browser: Chrome or Edge. Firefox cannot share a tab securely, so it is left out of this part. To present from your computer without going through the browser, there is also the [desktop app](/download), which shares a screen or a window directly.
- To **watch**, any browser or the app works, nothing to install.
- The film open in a tab (a streaming service, YouTube, a local video player) before you go live.

## Step by step, for the presenter

1. Join the voice call in the channel where everyone is.
2. In the call bar, click **Watch party**.
3. Chrome will ask which tab to share. Pick the tab with the film.
4. Check the quick checklist that appears before going live: browser, tab audio, quality, camera/mic if you are using them.
5. Pick a quality: 720p is the default, and capture reaches 60 frames per second when the connection can handle it. 1080p is there for whoever wants more detail.
6. Confirm. From there, everyone in the call sees the screen and hears that tab's audio.

To stop, it is the same **Watch party** button. Whoever is presenting can also hand off to a co-host without cutting the stream for anyone.

## Does the film's audio actually come through?

Yes, with no extra step: since you shared the Chrome or Edge tab the film is playing in, that tab's audio follows the video automatically. The **Broadcast** panel has a mixer with two controls, **My mic** and **Tab audio**: raise one, lower the other, and when you talk the film's audio ducks on its own and comes back to normal once you stop. Nobody has to touch anything mid-scene.

## How it works for whoever is just watching

Whoever just wants to watch clicks **Watch**, and that is it: no joining the call, no live microphone, nobody hearing breathing or background noise. The waiting screen says what is happening if the host has not started yet, is reconnecting, or the stream has stalled.

To comment by voice, there is a **Request to speak** button, and the host decides who gets a voice. You can leave the channel mid-film without losing the thread: the watch party shrinks into a corner of the screen and keeps playing while you browse another part of pqp.

A room with more than 10 people auto-mutes join and leave sounds on its own, with a button to bring them back if you want.

## Quality and stalls

If the picture stalls or the stream looks off for more than ten seconds, a warning shows up on screen for whoever is watching, instead of leaving everyone thinking it is their own connection's fault. If the presenter's microphone is accidentally muted, a strip stays visible on the bar until they notice and turn it on.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to install anything to run a watch party?**
No. To present from the browser, use Chrome or Edge (Firefox cannot share a tab securely, so it is left out). There is also a desktop app to present from. To just watch, any browser or the app works.

**Does the film's audio come through?**
Yes: when you share the Chrome or Edge tab the film is playing in, that tab's audio comes through automatically. A mixer lets you balance your microphone against the film's audio, and it ducks the film on its own while you talk.

**How many people can watch together?**
A watch party with over 500 people watching at the same time has already run on pqp. Whoever is watching does not need a microphone or to join the call to see it: they just click Watch.

**Can I ask to speak during the watch party?**
Yes. Anyone just watching has a Request to speak button, and the host decides who gets a voice on stage. The host can also hand off presenting to a co-host without cutting the stream.

**If I leave the channel, does the watch party stop?**
Not for you: it shrinks into a corner of the screen and keeps playing while you browse another part of pqp. For everyone else, the party keeps running as normal.
