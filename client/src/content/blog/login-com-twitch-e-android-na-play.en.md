## Sign in with Twitch

You can now create an account and log in with Twitch. The sign-in screen has a Twitch button next to the others. One tap, you approve it there, and you land in pqp.

If you already watch streamers' watch parties, it is the shortest way in: no new password.

This is the login. Linking your Twitch account to your profile, so it shows on your page, is still in Settings, Connections.

## Android on Google Play

The Android app is on Google Play. Search for pqp or open it from [pqp.gg/android](/android). The APK is still further down that page if you prefer it.

The Play version is 0.4.2. In it, watch parties have a stage on your phone, with the host's camera and four ways to view. And you can host from your own phone when the channel allows it.

## Calls that come back fast

When your internet dropped mid-call, the app took 40 to 60 seconds just to notice the connection was dead. Now it notices in about 10 and rejoins the room.

In a 3 second drop we simulated, it took about 9 seconds to be back. If the browser reports the network going away and coming back, it is 2 or 3 seconds after the network returns.

## Language on the public pages

At the top of the site, next to Log in, there is a language picker: Português with Brazil's flag, English with the UK's and Español with Spain's. Pick one, the page reloads and pqp remembers it.

## The rest

- When you focus one stream in a call, a Show all streams button appears. Esc goes back too.
- Someone who signs up from a community link while a watch party is live lands straight on it, without going through the server Overview.
- On Linux, a hint next to the share screen button says when a share will go out without sound. In Chrome, only a tab carries audio.
- On Windows 10, when the browser strips your computer's sound from a share, the notice now says the way out: share a Chrome tab with "Also share tab audio". The "(no sound)" you see on someone else's share explains it is the sharer's system.
- If you picked OBS Virtual Camera as your camera, a hint in voice settings says how to send just your face.
