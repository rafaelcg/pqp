## Conversations

The DM side was rebuilt and is now called Conversations.

The list has a preview of the last message, the time, a dot for who is online, the unread count and a "typing..." line. On top there is a search and the filters All, Unread and Groups. Pinned conversations sit under Pinned, the rest under Recent. When a message comes in, that conversation jumps to the top.

The door on the left rail changed too: it now says Chats. The red number counts conversations with something new, and the people who wrote to you show up as little faces under it.

**New conversation** lists people first: who is online right now, your friends and people you talked to recently. Tick one person and it opens the conversation. Tick several and it becomes a group.

Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) opens a "go to": type the name of a person, group, server or channel and press Enter. Message search moved to Ctrl+F (Cmd+F). When you accept a friend request, a card shows up with a Say hi button.

On a phone, in the browser and on iPhone, there is a bar at the bottom with Chats, Servers and You. The app opens on the last tab you used.

## The Baú in chat

Staff can send a Baú post to a channel. It goes as a card with the cover, the title, a piece of the text and a button, Open in Baú, instead of a bare link. The post menu has Share to a channel and Copy link. Pasted a Baú post link into a message? It also becomes a card, and whoever opens it lands right on the post.

In a Baú post, typing `#` opens the server's channel list, and the `#channel` becomes a link to it.

When a new post goes out, the server icon on the left rail gets a lime green number, and so does the Baú row. Inside the server, the latest post shows as a card in the sidebar. Once you see it, the card folds into a thin row with the thumbnail.

When you post a video, there is a Cover block: pick a frame from the video or upload an image.

On iPhone the Baú video opens full screen, turns with the phone and keeps playing in a small window when you leave the app. Staff can also post to the Baú from the app.

## Call audio that fixes itself

A microphone can stop sending sound without telling anyone: the bluetooth headset sleeps, the USB hiccups, the system switches device. The app showed everything normal and nobody heard you. Now it notices and tries to reopen the microphone on its own.

If that does not work, a card shows up in the corner: "Looks like your mic stopped", or "Can't hear anyone?" when the call's sound got stuck. The **Fix it** button turns the audio back on without leaving the call.

## Screen share at 60 fps

A 60 frame share got more bandwidth, because the limit used to be made for 30 and the picture dropped in resolution. And there is a new choice, only for whoever is sharing, in the call's quality options: **When the connection tightens**. Keep it smooth is what you had. Keep it sharp holds the resolution and lets the frame rate drop, which suits text and code better.

## The rest
