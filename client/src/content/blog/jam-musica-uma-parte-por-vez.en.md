Jam is here. Somebody records an idea, the others record their part on top, each from home, with or without the camera. The song plays as one band, in a mosaic of the people who played.

Public jams live at [jam.pqp.gg](https://jam.pqp.gg). You do not need an account to listen.

## How to use it

1. **Open a jam and press play.** Go to [jam.pqp.gg](https://jam.pqp.gg), pick a song and play it. Each square is a person, with their instrument. Tap a square to hear only that part. The **Falta alguém** tab ("somebody is missing") lists the songs that still ask for an instrument.
2. **Record the missing part.** The button is named for what is missing, like **Gravar o baixo** (record the bass). You make a free account, land straight back on that song, and put on headphones before you press the red button: the band plays in your ear and your take lines up in time. You can record video too, if you want. When you send it, whoever started the jam picks whether your part goes in the mix, or the jam takes it automatically if it was set up that way.
3. **Start your own.** In **Começar uma jam** (start a jam), you record an idea from home (a guitar, a voice, a beat) and say which instrument is still missing. Anyone can record on top. To get it onto jam.pqp.gg you choose **Tornar pública** (make public) on the song: each person who played answers whether their part can go, and anything unanswered stays out. Whoever started it decides what goes in the mix and can take the song down whenever they like.

Only post what you made or have the right to use. Your own music is the best way to start.

## Everybody records in their own time

Nobody plays at the same time, so this is not a live jam. Each part is recorded after the last one, over what is already there, and pqp compensates for your headphones' delay so your part lands in time. There are no effects and no metronome.

## Not yet

On phones (iPhone and Android) you can browse songs, listen, record an audio part, vote, and mix parts in or out. Editing a song, adjusting a take, recording with the camera, sharing, searching and reporting are still browser only. And public jams do not open in the app yet: the link opens the site.
