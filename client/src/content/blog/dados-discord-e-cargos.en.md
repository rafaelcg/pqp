A lot shipped this week. From now on, what changes in pqp gets written here. Not one post per change. When there is a pile, there is a post.

This is the first one in that shape.

## Dice, a coin, a deck, a poll

Chat now has `/roll`, `/flip`, `/choose`, `/draw` and `/shuffle`. The result is a card, not a loose line in the middle of the conversation.

The first time you open the app after this, a corner card introduces the pack. Once.

![The corner card: dice, a coin, a deck](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/novidade.webp "Dice, coin, deck. /roll, /flip or /draw. Next is the poll.")

`/roll 2d6+1d8 ! attack` works. A d20 hitting 20 or 1 tints the total. `/draw` pulls from a deck that belongs to that channel. `/shuffle` reshuffles it.

![Dice, a coin, and a card in chat](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/dados.webp "/roll 2d6+1d8 attack, /flip, /draw. Three cards in the same channel.")

Polls: `/poll` or the button. Two to ten options, a timer, single or multi. You see who voted. It is not anonymous. The author or a manager can close it.

![A poll in chat, Lobby ticked](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/enquete.webp "At the bottom: Onde a galera cai hoje. Lobby at 100%, one vote, 24 hours, Encerrar enquete.")

## Copy a Discord sidebar

When you create a community you can paste a `discord.new` link. pqp reads the template and shows what will be created, before it creates it.

![Preview of the Friends & Family template](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/discord.webp "This is what will be created. general, games, music, Lounge, Stream Room. Below that, what does not copy.")

Discord itself does not change. No bot, no Discord login, no people, no messages. Structure only.

If the template has cosmetic cargos or private channels, those come too. Announcement and forum become text. Stage becomes voice. Discord permission bits do not come across, because the bits are not the same.

## Cargos

Communities used to have owner, admin, member. Promote, demote, done. It was not enough.

Every community now has **Owner, Admin, Manager, Moderator and @everyone**. Higher on the list means more power. Only the owner of the community sits outside that.

Click the person and tick the cargo. Kick and ban are a different thing, lower on the card.

![Ticking Adm on a member card](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/ficha.gif "Bia. Adm is ticked. Remover and Banir stay separate, in red.")

A mod can kick someone below them. They cannot ban. A manager can ban and rename the community. They cannot delete it.

In Settings, Cargos: the list in order, and the colour.

![The cargos list and the colour picker](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/cargos.webp "Dono, Adm, Gerente, Mod, @everyone. Adm open on the colour picker.")

Drag to reorder, reset to the default if you want, and add people without opening every card.

Portuguese shows Dono, Adm, Gerente and Mod. If you already had a homemade cargo with those names, it stays yours.

## The member card

Click a name, in the list or in chat. The buttons are the same size, there is a crown or a shield next to the name, and below that: Depoimentos, Contas, Comunidades.

Search finds people by account name even when they have a nickname. The members dialog groups the same way as the sidebar: owner, cargos, online, offline, banned.

## The rest

If the microphone you had picked is gone (USB headset, Bluetooth, permissions reset), the call uses the default instead of dying on an English error.

![Microphone set to the system default](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/mic.webp "Settings, Voice and video. Microphone: system default.")

A page you can send: [pqp.gg/download](/download). Desktop and iPhone.

![The download page](/blog/dados-discord-e-cargos/download.webp "Download the app. Mac first, then Windows, Linux, iPhone on TestFlight.")

On iPhone, tap a friend to open the DM. You do not have to long-press any more.

Cameras show up in iPhone server calls, not only in a private call.

On Android you can react and send a file.

## Not yet

On iPhone the card still says Promote and Demote. Promote still makes someone Admin. Demote still strips Admin, Manager and Moderator. Ticking cargos is on the site and the desktop app.

The Turma dos 1000 medal exists and goes to nobody until the thousandth real human account. Numbers are not reused.

Watch party is not in the app yet. There is something behind the scenes. You cannot click it.

Sending your own screen from a phone is still out. Watching someone else's already works.
