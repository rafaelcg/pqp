## Drag a file anywhere

Until now you could only drop a file on a text channel's chat. Now you can drag and drop onto more places:

- the chat of a voice channel, even in the middle of a call, over the stage or the video;
- a thread;
- the Baú, to attach it to the post you are writing;
- your avatar, profile banner, the server icon and banner, and the community cover.

Drop a folder and pqp tells you it needs the files inside. If the server does not take uploads, the notice says so instead of staying quiet.

Dropping a file on a place that does not accept it also stopped opening the file in place of pqp. That holds in the browser and in the desktop app.

## Call controls that fade

With one screen in focus in a call, the controls bar stayed on top of the picture the whole time. One click on mute was enough: the button kept focus and the bar thought you were still using it. Now only the keyboard holds the bar. Stop moving and it fades. Move the mouse and it is back.

If you do not like it, turn it off in Settings, Voice & Video, Hide controls automatically.

## Waiting alone in a call

A call dropped you after 10 minutes alone. Now it waits 90. The warning still comes a minute before, and muting, opening the camera, sharing your screen or pressing the warning's button resets the count.

## Linux: your computer's sound without the call

In the Linux desktop app, you can share your screen with your computer's sound and without the call riding along. It works with Spotify and with games, including games running under Wine or Proton. It needs the desktop app from 0.2.3 on (0.2.4 fixed the games). In the Flatpak build that sound is still off.

## A game in exclusive fullscreen on Windows

When a game is in exclusive fullscreen, Windows does not hand the picture to the capture and viewers see nothing. The app now tells you right away and points the way. In CS2, for example: Settings, Video, Display mode, Fullscreen Windowed.

## Watch party

The presenter's camera has a button to hide it. It also moves together with the stream now, because before it ran behind the picture.

## New Settings

Settings were rebuilt. The side list now has Account, App and Support, and each tab has grouped rows, switches for on and off, and error messages in your language. Only Profile has a save: everything else applies at once, so the Save button in the footer is gone. In Profile, if you close with unsaved changes the app holds you and asks. Cmd or Ctrl+S saves.

## The rest

- There is a Help and contact in Settings and in your user menu. The button opens an email with the app version and your system already filled in, and nothing else of yours.
- On the sign-in and sign-up screens, the Portuguese text now sounds like the rest of pqp.
- On iPhone (TestFlight), watching a shared screen in a call turns the app to landscape.
