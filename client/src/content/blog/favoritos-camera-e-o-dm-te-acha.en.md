A lot shipped after the 30 August note. It piled up. Here it is.

## Baú

The community's place for posts that stay. Clips, images, PDFs, YouTube. Staff post. People like and comment. It is not chat, and it pings nobody.

The owner turns it on in community settings. It starts off.

There is a **pinned** post at the top. The row in the bar shows how many you have not seen.

Staff can schedule, save a draft, and preview. Some posts lock for the VIP cargo. Paying for that is not a thing yet: if the owner marked you VIP, you see them.

![A Baú post, with a photo, likes, and a comment](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/bau.jpg "Session post, a heart, and a comment underneath.")

On the site. Not on iPhone or Android yet.

## Star a channel, and VIP

Star a channel. It goes up to **Favorites**, at the top of the bar. Per community. Click again and it goes back.

VIP joins the ladder: Owner, Admin, Manager, Moderator, **VIP**, @everyone. Click the person and tick it. Homemade cargos sit with them.

![Favorites at the top of the bar, and the VIP card](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/favoritos.webp "Favorites, # general with the star. In the corner: Adm, Gerente, Mod, VIP.")

## One click and you are in voice

Click the channel. You are in. No phone tile on the side, no double-click.

In the call, whoever turned a camera on shows up on the stage. They do not vanish into a strip underneath.

The community nickname is what other people see in the call. Change the nick, it changes live.

## DMs that find you

A DM arrives: an in-app toast, a sound, and a number on the browser tab. Conversation rows now have a date, so you can tell today from last week.

## GIFs

On the site and on iPhone, the GIF button searches. Pick one, send it, it stays in chat. On Android a GIF animates if somebody sent one. There is no search button yet.

## Android, for real

[pqp.gg/android](/android). It is an APK. Play is not open yet. Chrome will warn you. That is expected.

![The Android page](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/android.webp "pqp on your Android, before the store. Download the APK.")

On the phone you can share your screen. In the phone browser, you cannot.

## If the call will not join

Settings, Voice and video, **Check connection**. Five checks. Copy the report and send it. Nothing is uploaded.

![The connection check, five checks](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/conexao.webp "Connection check. Server, token, realtime, STUN, TURN.")

If the microphone you had picked is gone, the call tries the others before giving up.

Screen share: if the machine audio never arrives, the picture still goes. The share does not die.

On Windows, the desktop app can now drop the call's own audio from a share. Electron would not let us before.

## If you missed it, from August

These shipped and never got their own note:

- Steam and Twitch on your profile. Settings, Connections. This is not how you sign into pqp. They show on the public page only if you allow it. Battle.net is not on yet.
- Sounds for mentions, voice, and calls. You can turn them off under Notifications.
- Two people can share a screen in the same call.
- On desktop, you pick the window. It is no longer a silent grab of the whole screen.

## The rest

A topic is a real thread, the composer does not eat what you were typing, and the member list hoists staff at the top.

A DM can be **pinned to the rail**, under the Home bubble, above the halls. Their face stays there. Click it and it opens.

## Not yet

Watch party is still not something you can click. There is work behind it.

Sending your own screen from iPhone is still out. Watching someone else's already works. On Android, sending already works.
