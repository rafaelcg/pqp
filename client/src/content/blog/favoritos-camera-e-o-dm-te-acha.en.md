## Baú

The community's place for posts that stay. Clips, images, PDFs, YouTube. Staff post. People like and comment. It is not chat, and it pings nobody.

The owner turns it on in community settings. It starts off.

There is a **pinned** post at the top. The row in the bar shows how many you have not seen.

Staff can schedule, save a draft, and preview. Some posts lock for the VIP cargo. Paying for that is not a thing yet: if the owner marked you VIP, you see them.

![A Baú post, with a photo, likes, and a comment](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/bau.jpg "Session post, a heart, and a comment underneath.")

On the site. Not on iPhone or Android yet.

## Pin a DM to the rail

A DM can be **pinned to the rail**, under the Home bubble, above the halls. Their face stays there. Click it and it opens. Cap is 15.

## If you missed it, from August

These shipped and never got their own note:

- Steam and Twitch on your profile. Settings, Connections. This is not how you sign into pqp. They show on the public page only if you allow it. Battle.net is not on yet.
- Sounds for mentions, voice, and calls. You can turn them off under Notifications.
- Two people can share a screen in the same call.
- On desktop, you pick the window. It is no longer a silent grab of the whole screen.
