The Baú shipped. And you can pin a DM to the rail.

## Baú

Clips, photos, PDFs, YouTube. Staff post, people like and comment. It stays. It does not vanish in chat.

The owner turns it on in community settings. It starts off.

You can keep one post at the top. The row in the bar shows how many you have not seen yet.

Staff can schedule, save a draft, preview. Some posts are only for people the owner marked VIP. Paying is not a thing yet.

![A Baú post, with a photo, likes, and a comment](/blog/favoritos-camera-e-o-dm-te-acha/bau.jpg "Session post, a heart, and a comment underneath.")

On iPhone and Android you can read, like, and comment. Posting, scheduling, and drafts are still on the site.

## DMs on the rail

Right-click a conversation, **Pin to the rail**. Their face sits under the Home bubble, above the halls. Click it and it opens. Up to 15. Site and desktop for now.

## From August

- Steam and Twitch on your profile: Settings, Connections. This is not how you sign into pqp. They show on the public page only if you allow it. Battle.net is not on yet.
- Sounds for mentions, voice, and calls. You can turn them off under Notifications.
- Two people sharing a screen in the same call.
- On desktop you pick the window. It does not grab the whole screen without asking.
