pqp always had its code on GitHub, but it had no licence. Without one, "open source" is just a nice phrase: legally nobody could copy, run or modify any of it. That is fixed.

## AGPL-3.0

The repository is now under AGPL-3.0. In practice:

- You can run pqp on your own server, for your own people, forever, for free.
- You can modify anything you like.
- If you run a modified version and offer it to other people over a network, you publish your modifications too.

That last line is why it is AGPL and not MIT. A chat app is a service that runs on a server, and AGPL is the licence that closes exactly that gap.

Code at [github.com/rafaelcg/pqp](https://github.com/rafaelcg/pqp).

## A feedback box inside the app

Complaining by DM works badly and disappears. There is a box under settings now: write what broke, send it, it reaches me.

A confirmed bug becomes a **bug-hunter badge** on your public profile. It is an honest counter: it only counts what I actually confirmed and fixed.

## Why this is a release note and not a blog post

Because both things change what you can do with the project. One gives you the right to take pqp with you if I disappear. The other gives you a way to tell me before that happens.
