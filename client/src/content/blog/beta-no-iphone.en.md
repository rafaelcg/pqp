The iPhone app has left the closed circle. Anyone with the link can join the beta through TestFlight, with no individual invite and no queue.

## What already works

- Voice in rooms, on the same São Paulo server the browser uses.
- Text, attachments, and the communities you are already in.
- **Watching** a screen share. If somebody on a computer is sharing, you can watch from your phone.
- Notifications for DMs, mentions and calls.
- Invite links open straight into the app when it is installed.

## What does not, yet

- **Sending** your iPhone screen. You can watch, you cannot broadcast.
- Screen share still goes without sound, on phone and desktop alike. That is true for everybody today and it is the next thing in the queue.

## Why TestFlight and not the App Store

Because saying App Store would be a lie. The app is in beta, TestFlight is exactly where Apple wants a beta to live, and builds expire every 90 days. When it reaches the store we will write that here, and not before.

Installing takes two taps: [pqp.gg/beta](/beta), the blue button, TestFlight does the rest.

## Found a bug

There is a feedback box inside the app now, under settings. A confirmed bug earns a bug-hunter badge on your profile. That is not a joke, it is the cheapest way to find out what breaks on hardware I do not own.
