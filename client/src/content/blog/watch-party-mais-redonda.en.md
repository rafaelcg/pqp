A round of watch party fixes, mostly on iPhone.

- The movie no longer goes black when you turn your phone.
- With the phone sideways, the extra back button and the channel name over the movie are gone.
- The host's camera no longer freezes in side by side.
- Viewers no longer hear the host's voice twice.
- The mute button and the camera options now stay on screen, including in your phone's browser.

The first three are in the iPhone app: they are on TestFlight now and reach the App Store with the next update.
